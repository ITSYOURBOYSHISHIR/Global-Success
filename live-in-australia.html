Why Australia

Main features of Australia

What makes Australia a top choice for international students.

Sixth-largest country

Tropical north to cool southern cities — every student can find a destination that fits.

3,000+ hours of sunlight

Outdoor lifestyle, beaches, parks, and community events year-round in many regions.

Quality of life

Strong minimum wage, Fair Work protections, and work-life balance across industries.

Multicultural cities

Nepalese communities in every major city — support networks are closer than you think.

19 UNESCO World Heritage sites

From the Great Barrier Reef to historic landmarks worth exploring on breaks.

Unique wildlife

Kangaroos, koalas, and thousands of species found nowhere else on Earth.

Free food support nationwide

Salvation Army, Foodbank, and community kitchens — our Free Food Tips package maps them for you.

Did you know?

10 curiosities about Australia

Quick facts that surprise most new arrivals.

01

Kangaroo paradise

There are roughly twice as many kangaroos as people — around 50 million.

02

Canberra is the capital

Not Sydney or Melbourne — Canberra was purpose-built in 1913 to settle the rivalry.

03

10,000+ beaches

Visiting one per day would take over 27 years to see them all.

04

Unique wildlife

About 83% of mammals and many plants exist only in Australia.

05

Christmas on the beach

December is summer — barbecues and beach days replace snow and winter coats.

06

Drive on the left

A British legacy — our Driving Class package covers Australian road rules before you fly.

07

The Dingo Fence

At 5,614 km, it is one of the longest structures on Earth — longer than the Great Wall of China.

08

200+ languages

More than 200 languages are spoken in Australian homes — one of the most multicultural nations.

09

World-class trams

Melbourne has the largest tram network outside Europe — free in the CBD zone.

10

Great Barrier Reef

The world's largest coral reef system — 2,300 km long and visible from space.
